# Code of Conduct

English · [Español](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.es.md)

RCleaner is built by people with different backgrounds, languages and levels of
experience. We want taking part to be a good experience for everyone, whether
you're filing your first issue or reviewing your hundredth pull request.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers and people writing in a
  language that isn't their first.
- Assume good intent and ask before concluding.
- Give feedback about the work, not the person. Explain the *why*.
- Accept decisions and feedback gracefully, and say so when you're wrong.
- Keep discussions on topic and help others find answers.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory jokes or language about anyone's identity, background,
  appearance, ability or beliefs.
- Sexualized language or imagery.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Repeatedly pushing a point after being asked to stop, or deliberately
  derailing discussions.
- Any other behavior that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This applies to every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions,
reviews, chats) and to anyone representing the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

If something happens that makes you or someone else uncomfortable, contact the
maintainers privately through the contact options on the maintainers' GitHub
profiles or through a private security advisory on the repository
(**Security → Report a vulnerability**), which only maintainers can read.
Reports are handled confidentially and promptly.

## Enforcement

Maintainers decide on the response, in proportion to what happened: a private
note, a public warning, a temporary ban or a permanent ban from the project.
Maintainers who don't follow this code may be removed from the role.
